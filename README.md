# Celestial Lab

Exploring space often means moving between separate trackers, catalogues and visualisations. Celestial Lab brings seven related space experiences into one navigable application, letting users move from satellites and launches to the Sun, asteroids and distant planets without losing a familiar workspace.

## Tech stack

| Layer | Technologies |
|---|---|
| Core stack | PHP; WordPress; JavaScript; HTML; CSS |

## Explore satellites and people in space

Satellite Map presents supported orbital catalogues and a separate Starlink view, while People in Space adds a human perspective to current spaceflight. Pan, zoom and fullscreen controls support moving from an overview to an object of interest. Satellite positions are predictions from published orbital elements, not measured live locations.

## Follow launches

Launch tracking helps users browse upcoming activity and examine the available schedule information. Date precision is kept meaningful: a confirmed day and a broader estimated window are different kinds of information. The views provide a place to return as published schedules develop.

## Observe the Sun and space weather

Sun Live and space-weather areas bring solar observations, solar-wind information, aurora-related views and available alerts together. Users can explore the relationship between the different displays while retaining the source and observation context. Guides help make the specialised information more approachable.

## Investigate asteroids

Asteroid Watch separates encounter information, impact-monitoring entries and fireball observations. Its encounter view gives users a way to compare published timing, distance and velocity. Estimates and source limitations remain part of understanding the objects being shown.

## Explore planets beyond the Solar System

Exoplanet Explorer offers another scale of space exploration. Distance views distinguish physical scaling from an explicitly expanded nearby view, helping users understand the purpose of the presentation. It is a way to explore a catalogue without mistaking a display choice for a different physical distance.

## Move between the experiences

Shared navigation brings the seven modules and their available views into a consistent application. Display preferences, navigation hiding and fullscreen help adapt the workspace to the current activity and screen. Missing or unavailable source information is surfaced rather than replaced with invented observations.
